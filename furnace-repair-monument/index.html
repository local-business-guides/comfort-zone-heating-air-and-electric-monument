What to prepare before a service visit

A little preparation makes it easier to explain the problem clearly. Before calling or meeting a technician, consider this short checklist:

  • Note when the issue began and whether it happens every time the furnace runs.
  • Write down any unusual sounds or changes in how the system operates.
  • Check whether the thermostat setting matches the temperature you want.
  • Share what you have already tried, including any changes you made.
  • Keep the area around the furnace accessible if you can do so safely.

Avoid taking the system apart or repeatedly changing settings to test it. Don’t assume a sound or a short heating cycle points to one specific repair. The technician can assess the equipment and explain what they find before you decide what to do next.
