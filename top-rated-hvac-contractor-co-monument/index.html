Get ready for a useful service visit

A little preparation helps keep the conversation focused. Before the technician arrives, gather what you know about the system and make the equipment accessible if you can do so safely. There’s no need to move or handle equipment yourself.

A helpful preparation list includes:

  • What the thermostat is set to and what appears on its display.
